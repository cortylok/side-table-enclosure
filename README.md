# Side Table Playground

[![Open the playground](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E2%96%B6%20%20Open%20the%20playground-2d5a84?style=for-the-badge)](https://cortylok.github.io/side-table-enclosure/)

**Click the blue button.** The playground opens in your browser, on a computer or a phone. You do not need to install anything.

## How to use it

- **Turn the view:** drag on an empty space (on a phone: two fingers).
- **Zoom:** mouse wheel (on a phone: pinch).
- **Move a part:** drag the side door, the drawer front, the phone bed or the S3 carriage, then let go.
- **Part details:** tap or click a part.
- The menu on the right (on a phone: the icon at the top right) has the views, sections and settings.

It loads its 3D library from the internet, so you need a connection.
